How To Play

Power-Ups

🧨 Multi-ball – Spawn extra balls

📏 Expand – Paddle gets wider

🐢 Slow – Slows balls

❤️ Extra Life – +1 life

⚡ Speed – Faster ball

Brick Types

🟢 Green – 1 hit

🔵 Ice – 2 quick hits

🔴 Red – 3 hits

🟡 Gold – Drops power-up

🟣 Purple – Boss brick
