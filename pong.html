⚽ Ultimate Soccer Career

How To Play

Career Mode

You start with 10 Million budget.

Buy players from transfer market.

Sell players anytime.

Upgrade stadium to earn more prize money.

Sign sponsors for bonus income.

Playing Matches

You control ONE player from your team.

Use WASD to move.

Press SPACE to shoot.

Score more goals than opponent before time ends.

League System

Each season has 5 matches.

Win = 3 points.

Draw = 1 point.

Top of league wins trophy + money.

Player Growth

Your controlled player improves when scoring goals.
